# Unknown Neighbor

# Description

The Unknown Neighbor is not a monster that came to the town. It is the town.

At a distance — and in the fog of Red Valve, distance is most of what you get —
it is a resident. Same coat, same tired walk, same face you passed on the way to
the workshop. The tells are there, but you have to want to see them: a thin root
running under the skin of the neck, and a faint outline of light around the whole
body — the colour of whatever strain took that particular person — pulsing on a
heartbeat that is not the neighbour's. On a quiet street it is the only thing
moving.

Get closer, or put a bullet in it, and the person comes apart in stages. The
skull splits into a crown of petals with a core burning at the centre. One
forearm unfolds into a scythe of bone. The ribs hinge open and four legs unpack
from the back. By the last stage the human body is still there, whole, walking —
it is simply no longer the thing in charge.

# Lore: "Folded, Not Replaced"

The Red Valve did not pump gas into the town the way it pumped it into the mines.
Above ground the dose was thinner, slower, and the mineral did something worse
than it did to the Huskers.

The Cobalt Huskers were **overwritten**: bone hardened to black stone, mind
unified into one screaming consciousness. They stopped being miners in the same
hour they stopped breathing air.

The people of the town were **folded**. The mineral did not replace them. It
grew inside the space they already occupied, in the geometry the Huskers first
broke open — the geometry where a thing can be inside another thing without
either one getting bigger. The neighbour kept their body, their gait, the route
they walked every evening. Something else simply moved in underneath, took up no
room at all, and waited.

That is why they keep walking the same streets. It is not mimicry and it is not
a trap. It is the last of the routine of a person who is still, technically,
there. The unfolding happens when the thing underneath decides it needs the body
more than the disguise — and it decides that when the body starts taking damage.

# The Four Strains

The mineral did not settle the same way in everybody. Four strains took hold in
the town, and each one wears a different crown.

| Strain | Colour | Crown | Behaviour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pale** | bone white, cold blue light | long smooth petals | fastest, thinnest, dies soonest |
| **Bile** | yellow-green | petals hung with dripping bulbs | the common one; the town is mostly this |
| **Crimson** | deep red | short petals ringed with spines | heaviest, hits hardest, folds least |
| **Ash** | grey, cracked, ember light | three thick horns, a sparse crown | folds through space constantly; its husk holds longest |

# What It Does

- **The Choir** — it plants itself, the crown opens all the way, and what comes
  out is not one scream but many, in voices that belonged to people. The air
  folds and runs outward along the ground as a ring. It does not chase you and
  you cannot shoot it down; you get out of the ring, or you get behind
  something.
- **The Blind Harvest** — it folds out of the world and three of it fold back in
  around you, all in the same striking pose. Two are images. One is not.
- **The Sowing** — it tears off its own hand and throws it. Where the hand lands
  it plants, blooms, and holds you where you stand while it drains. The hand
  grows back. Shoot the bloom to get free.
- **The Husk** (defence) — it unfolds its own skin outward into ten plates of
  person that orbit it and eat the damage. Every hit tears one plate away, so
  you can see exactly how much of it is left. Tear off the last one and the
  husk bursts.

# Taking It Apart

Arms, hands, legs and head each come off on their own, and each one changes the
fight. Take the right arm and the scythe goes with it. Take the left hand and it
cannot sow until the hand grows back. Take a leg and it drags.

Take the **head** and you get the worst trade in Red Valve: the disguise is over,
the parasite takes the body on the spot and jumps straight to its final form —
but it is now blind. It keeps attacking, only at where you *were* a second ago.

# Where To Find Them

Everywhere. They walk the streets of the town in Chapter 1 alongside the zombies,
the Cobalt Husker and the shadows, and there are always several of them, because
one neighbour alone would not fool anyone.

They only fold through space inside the arena, and they will not fold onto ground
that is not there — the platform has an edge, and whatever is underneath it is
not part of the fight. On the street they just walk toward you, at the pace of
someone who knows you, and the outline goes out the moment they stop pretending.
